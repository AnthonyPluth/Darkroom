Safelight, dark: tokens

One theme, dark only. Each value becomes a Tailwind 4 @theme variable, so utilities such as bg-canvas, text-ink-muted and border-line exist. Status colours are only ever used with a text label and their own shape. Body text pairs are at least 4.5:1 (ink-muted on surface is 7.0:1).
